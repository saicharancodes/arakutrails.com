Vizag, Araku & Lambasingi 3N/4D

An exhilarating 4-day expedition across Andhra Pradesh’s top hill stations. Experience sub-zero chill in Lambasingi, witness the ethereal floating ocean of clouds at Vanjangi sunrise, and explore all of Araku Valley and Vizag coast.

Tour Overview

Step into the surreal wonderland of Andhra Pradesh. Known as the "Kashmir of Andhra", Lambasingi is renowned for recording near-freezing temperatures, misty pine forests, strawberry and apple plantations. Combined with the magical sunrise at Vanjangi Hills—where clouds float below your feet like waves—this 3 Nights / 4 Days circuit is an unforgettable adventure.

Complete with 4x4 jeep transfers to sunrise viewpoints, campfire tent camping, resort stays, and visits to Borra Caves, waterfalls, and Vizag beaches.

4-Day Detailed Itinerary

DAY 1 • VIZAG ARRIVAL & SIGHTSEEING

Coastal Highlights & Vizag Stay

Pickup from Vizag airport/station. Visit the Submarine Museum, TU-142 Aircraft Museum, Kailasagiri Ropeway, and Rishikonda Beach. Overnight comfortable stay in Vizag.

DAY 2 • ARAKU VALLEY EXPEDITION

Borra Caves, Coffee Estates & Tribal Bonfire

Drive through Ananthagiri Ghats to the ancient million-year-old Borra Caves. Tour Padmapuram Gardens and Chaparai cascades. Check-in at Araku resort, followed by night bonfire & Dhimsa dance.

DAY 3 • VANJANGI SUNRISE & LAMBASINGI CHILL

Ocean of Clouds & Kashmir of Andhra Pradesh

Early 04:30 AM departure to Vanjangi Hills to catch the awe-inspiring 05:30 AM sunrise above rolling ocean clouds. Continue to Lambasingi, visit apple orchards, strawberry farms, and Kothapalli Waterfalls. Overnight bonfire tent/cottage stay in Lambasingi.

DAY 4 • PINE FORESTS & RETURN TO VIZAG

Misty Morning Walk & Drop-off

Wake up in crisp mountain air with a walk through dense pine plantations and local spice markets. Drive back to Visakhapatnam with late afternoon drop-off at railway station or airport.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the best time to see the Vanjangi ocean of clouds?
The cloud ocean phenomenon is best experienced between October and March at sunrise (5:30 AM to 7:00 AM).
How cold does Lambasingi get during winter?
Lambasingi temperatures frequently drop to 0°C to 5°C in December and January, earning it the title 'Kashmir of Andhra Pradesh'. Heavy woollens are recommended.
Is tent camping safe in Lambasingi and Araku?
Yes, all tent camping sites are fully enclosed, guarded, equipped with private campfire pits and western sanitation facilities.
