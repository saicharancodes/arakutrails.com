The Philosophy

About Araku Trails

We believe the true custodians of the Eastern Ghats are the communities who have cultivated and protected these mountains for generations.

Who We Are

Araku Trails began with a straightforward realization: mainstream commercial tourism frequently bypasses the very people whose lands and heritage make Araku Valley special. Online travel agencies sell packages without knowing the guides or the villages.

We built Araku Trails to invert that dynamic. Our digital platform serves as the direct booking bridge for local tribal guides, homestay hosts, and organic coffee smallholders in the Ananthagiri, Vanjangi, and Araku highlands.

Our Three Commitments

1. Direct Economic Impact

80%+ of booking revenue remains directly in the village hamlets with the guides and families hosting your journey.

2. Cultural Dignity

No staged performances or intrusive photography. Travellers join as invited guests in authentic community settings.

3. Safety & Trust

Experienced hill chauffeurs and mountain-trained guides with deep knowledge of weather, ghat terrain, and emergency protocols.
