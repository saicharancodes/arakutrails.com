Araku Serenity 1 Night / 2 Days

Immerse yourself in mountain tranquility with an overnight luxury resort stay in Araku Valley, warm campfire evenings accompanied by traditional tribal Dhimsa dance, coffee museum, and relaxed valley exploration.

Tour Overview

Slow down and breathe in fresh mountain mist. The Araku Serenity 1 Night / 2 Days getaway gives you ample time to soak in the charm of Araku Valley without rushing. Enjoy comfortable valley accommodations, pleasant twilight walks, bonfire nights, and cultural performances under star-studded skies.

Ideal for couples, families, and friends seeking relaxation combined with sightseeing of Borra Caves, waterfalls, tribal museums, and aromatic coffee gardens.

2-Day Detailed Itinerary

DAY 1 • 07:00 AM – 10:00 AM

Pickup from Vizag & Ghat Drive

Chauffeur pickup from Vizag. Ascend through mist-shrouded Ananthagiri hills with stops at viewpoint photography spots.

DAY 1 • 10:30 AM – 01:00 PM

Borra Caves & Coffee Tasting

Tour the subterranean chambers of Borra Caves, followed by fresh brewed organic Araku coffee at local plantation outlets.

DAY 1 • 02:00 PM – 05:00 PM

Resort Check-In & Chaparai Cascades

Check-in at your valley resort. Refresh and head out to Chaparai rocky stream cascades to dip your feet in cold mountain waters.

DAY 1 • 07:00 PM – 10:00 PM

Tribal Bonfire & Authentic Dhimsa Dance

Gather around the warm resort campfire. Enjoy an authentic live performance of the traditional Dhimsa tribal dance and dinner.

DAY 2 • 07:30 AM – 10:30 AM

Valley Morning & Padmapuram Gardens

Enjoy breakfast amidst misty valley greens. Visit the hanging tree-houses and flower varieties in Padmapuram Gardens.

DAY 2 • 11:00 AM – 02:00 PM

Araku Tribal Museum & Coffee House

Learn about the lifestyle, artifacts, and handicrafts of indigenous Eastern Ghats tribes. Experience the history of Araku coffee.

DAY 2 • 03:00 PM – 07:30 PM

Return Drive & Drop-off at Vizag

Comfortable afternoon drive descending the Ghat road back to Visakhapatnam railway station or hotel.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is hotel stay included in this 1N/2D package?
Yes, 1 night stay in an AP Tourism or verified private hillside resort with private balcony and valley views is included.
Can we witness the tribal Dhimsa dance at the resort?
Yes, an authentic tribal bonfire and cultural Dhimsa dance performance is arranged in the evening at the resort compound for guests.
How do we reach Katiki Waterfalls?
Katiki waterfalls requires a thrilling 7km off-road jeep drive through natural stream beds. Jeeps are arranged at the Borra railway gate junction.
