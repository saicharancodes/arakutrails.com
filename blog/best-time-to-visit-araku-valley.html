Best Time to Visit Araku Valley: Month-by-Month Weather & Highlights

By Araku Trails Editorial Desk October 15, 2026 5 min read

Perched at an elevation of 911 meters (2,989 feet) in the Eastern Ghats of Andhra Pradesh, Araku Valley is famous for pleasant hill breezes, aromatic organic coffee plantations, and mist-covered valleys. While Araku is a year-round getaway from coastal Visakhapatnam, knowing seasonal highlights helps you pick the perfect time for your travel style.

1. Winter (October to March) — The Peak Season

Average Temperature: 8°C – 25°C • Ideal for: Sightseeing, Vanjangi Sunrise, Bonfires & Camping

Winter is unquestionably the most magical season in Araku Valley. The air is crisp, early mornings are wrapped in thick fog, and evenings around a resort campfire with tribal Dhimsa dance are unforgettable.

  • Vanjangi Cloud Ocean: Between November and February, hikers can witness the spectacular sunrise at Vanjangi Hills where clouds float beneath your feet.
  • Coffee Harvest: Red coffee berries ripen across Ananthagiri hills, filling the air with fragrance.
  • Chilly Lambasingi: Nearby Lambasingi records temperatures as low as 0°C to 3°C in December and January.

2. Monsoon (July to September) — Lush Greenery & Waterfalls

Average Temperature: 20°C – 28°C • Ideal for: Waterfall Lovers, Photography & Nature Walks

The monsoon paints the Eastern Ghats in emerald greens. Cascading waterfalls like Katiki, Tatipudi, and Chaparai rapids flow at full force. Rain showers are intermittent, making road trips through the Ananthagiri Ghat road breathtakingly beautiful.

3. Summer (April to June) — A Quick Weekend Escape

Average Temperature: 22°C – 35°C • Ideal for: Avoiding Crowds & Budget Travel

While daytime temperatures can warm up, Araku remains 6°C to 8°C cooler than coastal Visakhapatnam, offering an accessible weekend refuge. Evenings remain breezy and comfortable for exploring Borra Caves and Padmapuram Gardens.
