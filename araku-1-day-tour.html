Araku Valley 1-Day Sightseeing

Explore the crown jewel of the Eastern Ghats in a comfortable private day trip. Wander through ancient million-year-old Borra Caves, botanical wonders, cascading water streams, and lush aromatic coffee gardens.

Tour Overview

Our 1-Day Araku Valley Tour is crafted for travelers who want to experience the best highlights of Araku in a single day with zero stress. Starting early from Visakhapatnam, you will cruise along the scenic Ananthagiri Ghats, visit the world-renowned Borra Caves, and immerse yourself in the rich tribal culture and fresh organic coffee plantations of Araku Valley.

Every tour includes a dedicated local sanitized private vehicle with a well-mannered driver fluent in regional routes, ensuring maximum comfort and punctuality.

Detailed Itinerary

06:00 AM – 06:30 AM

Pickup from Vizag Hotel / Station

Our chauffeur arrives at your designated hotel, railway station, or airport in Visakhapatnam to kickstart the scenic Ghat drive.

09:30 AM – 11:30 AM

Borra Caves Exploration

Marvel at the deepest limestone and speleothem cave formations in India with illuminated stalactites and stalagmites.

12:00 PM – 01:15 PM

Ananthagiri Coffee Plantations & Viewpoint

Walk through fragrant arabica coffee plantations and capture panoramic valley viewpoints enveloped in misty clouds.

01:30 PM – 02:30 PM

Authentic Lunch & Bamboo Chicken Tasting

Enjoy authentic Andhra regional meals and sample famous woodfire bamboo chicken cooked by Araku tribal chefs.

02:45 PM – 04:30 PM

Tribal Museum & Padmapuram Gardens

Discover the living tribal heritage of indigenous tribes and take a relaxing stroll through the hanging cottages of Padmapuram Botanical Gardens.

05:00 PM – 06:00 PM

Chaparai Water Cascades

Relax by the smooth rocky stream bed and gentle water ripples surrounded by dense green forest foliage.

06:30 PM – 09:30 PM

Return Drive & Vizag Drop-off

Scenic sunset descent along the Ghat road and drop-off back at your chosen hotel or Vizag railway station.
