Living Heritage

Village Ethno-Tourism Experiences

Participate in cultural, culinary, and artisanal traditions hosted directly by Araku Valley indigenous families.

Evening Ritual • 2 Hours

Dhimsa Folk Dance & Bonfire

An authentic evening gathering in the village square. Witness Mori flute melodies and rhythmic Dappu drums, followed by a shared open woodfire where visitors are warmly taught the traditional community step.

Agronomy • Half Day

Heirloom Coffee Roasting & Cupping

Walk alongside family cultivators under ancient canopy trees. Hand-harvest ripe cherries, de-pulp green beans, roast them in earthen clay pots over wood embers, and savor fresh mountain-brewed coffee.

Culinary • 3 Hours

Traditional Tribal Millet Cuisine

Prepare authentic valley delicacies including Ragi Sankati (finger millet balls), wild mountain greens cooked with country lentils, and famous bamboo-steamed chicken (Bongu Lo Kura) with local women cooks.

Artisan • 2.5 Hours

Forest Bamboo Craft & Weaving

Sit with master artisans to understand sustainable mountain bamboo harvesting. Shape natural bamboo fibres into woven baskets, decorative lanterns, and utility tools to take home directly from the creator.
