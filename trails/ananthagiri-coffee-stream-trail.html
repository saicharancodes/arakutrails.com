Ananthagiri Coffee & Stream Path

Follow tranquil hill streams through heirloom Arabica plantations with local tribal cultivators.

Trail Overview

The Ananthagiri hills harbor some of the oldest organic shade-grown Arabica coffee plots in South India. Under silver oak and jackfruit canopies, indigenous farming families have cultivated coffee without chemical fertilizers for generations.

Guided by Somanna and fellow growers, you walk alongside gentle natural streams, learn to identify medicinal forest herbs, roast green coffee beans over wood embers, and savor lunch steamed inside native green bamboo stems.

Trail Safety & Access Realities

  • Fitness Level: Easy to Moderate. Gentle undulating dirt trails and one natural shallow stream crossing. Suitable for active families.
  • Mobile Signal: Good Airtel and Jio 4G signal along most parts of Ananthagiri.
  • Toilet Facilities: Available at the starting coffee grower cooperative facility.
  • Medical Proximity: S. Kota Primary Health Centre is 18 km away; Vizag city hospitals are 75 km away.
  • What to Bring: Walking shoes with wet-rock traction, insect repellant (monsoon season), sun protection, and a refillable bottle.
